# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

## Hawaiian Rummy Scorekeeper

Hawaiian Rummy Scorekeeper respects your privacy.

This app is designed to keep score for Hawaiian Rummy games and does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

## Information Collection

This app:

- Does not require an account.
- Does not collect personal information.
- Does not track users.
- Does not use analytics.
- Does not send data to external servers.

## Data Storage

Game scores and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in storage technologies. This information never leaves your device.

## Third Parties

This app does not share information with third parties.
